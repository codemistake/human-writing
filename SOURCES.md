# Sources and attribution

This skill is an original synthesis informed by three MIT-licensed projects. It does not simply concatenate their prompt files.

## Anbeeld / WRITING.md

Repository: https://github.com/Anbeeld/WRITING.md

Used concepts:
- write for medium, task, reader, and evidence;
- preserve meaning during edits;
- direct openings and endings;
- supported specificity;
- avoid repeated model-level structures;
- no fake humanity;
- persistent lightweight global rules.

Reviewed current `WRITING-mini.md` from the main branch on 2026-09-06.

## ilyautov / humanizer-ru

Repository: https://github.com/ilyautov/humanizer-ru

Reviewed `skills/humanizer-ru/SKILL.md` v3.17.0 on 2026-09-06.

Used concepts:
- Russian-specific bureaucratic and translation-like constructions;
- genre-dependent editing intensity;
- false-positive handling;
- voice calibration;
- preserve facts while changing rhythm and wording;
- focus on noun-heavy bureaucratic phrasing, repetitive structure, chatbot artifacts, and Russian register.

Detector-evasion objectives and deterministic detector scoring were intentionally not carried over.

## blader / humanizer

Repository: https://github.com/blader/humanizer

Reviewed `SKILL.md` v2.11.2 on 2026-09-06.

Used concepts:
- inflated significance;
- sales language;
- vague attribution;
